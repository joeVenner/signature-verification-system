## [2026-10-03] Context Update
- **What changed:** Replaced the legacy hand-weighted verifier (v2) with v3: photometric normalisation (polarity, NLM denoise, 2-pass flat-field), SIFT+RANSAC keypoint and gradient-grid shape similarity, a fitted 3-parameter logistic fusion, and feature-wise max over specimens. Added validated logit ACCEPT/REVIEW/REJECT bands, a signature quality gate (INCONCLUSIVE), evidence-based explanations, a single pipeline orchestrator, a composed-cheque demo, API hardening, a determinism control point, and a leak-free benchmark suite (1:1, 3-specimen, robustness, cheque path).
- **Why:** The legacy benchmark under-used the data (76 distinct images) and was inflated by a dataset shortcut: CEDAR forgeries were scanned on whiter paper (paper brightness alone gives AUC 1.000). Honest skilled EER went 27.5% → 15.2% (1:1); with 3 specimens, skilled forgeries auto-accepted on held-out writers went from 3/96 to 0/96 while genuine auto-accept went 65% → 85%.
- **Impact:** src/preprocessing/{normalization,quality,form_cleaning}.py, src/verification/{features,similarity,explanation,deterministic}.py, src/adjudication/{thresholds,decision_engine}.py, src/pipeline.py, src/core/{config,types,determinism}.py, src/api/app.py, scripts/{demo_cli,demo_showcase}.py, benchmark/*, tests/{test_multi_reference,test_pipeline}.py, DETERMINISM.md.
- **Reference:** benchmark/experiments.md (EXP-000 … EXP-013), benchmark/REPORT.md. Not a git repo, so there is no commit.

### Retired approaches (with reasons)
- Legacy hesitation/ink-darkness features: no writer identity (random AUC 0.54); they measured the scan session.
- ECC-aligned correlation: unstable across writer folds (AUC 0.79 vs 0.90).
- Median/bilateral/Gaussian pre-filters: robustness gains, but accuracy dropped.
- Contrast stretch before denoise: fixes very low contrast but costs random EER 2.7% → 4.4–5.7%; the quality gate handles it instead.
- Iterative crop-box growth: chained along dashed borders and swallowed the whole signing area.
- Chance-corrected keypoint inliers: worse EER.

### Deferred / future ideas
- Larger evaluation data (full CEDAR 55 writers, GPDS, MCYT) and real cheques with enrolled specimens.
- Scale ×0.5 / rotation ≥ 10° robustness (multi-scale SIFT or orientation normalisation), measured with `benchmark/robustness.py`.
- Look-alike different-author signatures (synthetic writer B), which need finer stroke-order or curvature features.
- Production security: auth, rate limiting, HMAC-anchored audit chain, write locking, pinned lockfile.
- Business option: ACCEPT = top-5 impostor mean + 1 gives +9 pts genuine auto-accept at an unresolvable FAR difference (EXP-012).

### Retired approaches (with reasons)
- Piecewise (3-part) ICP, finer gradient grids, topology counts (junctions / endpoints / Euler number): no gain once direction + slant + profiles were fused.

### Deferred / future ideas

- Per-request concurrency limit / rate limiting; validate `evidence_reference.json` at load.
- Re-run the dataset-dependent tests once the original sample images are available.
- Full CEDAR beyond 6 + 6 images per writer, plus GPDS / MCYT, to tighten confidence intervals.
